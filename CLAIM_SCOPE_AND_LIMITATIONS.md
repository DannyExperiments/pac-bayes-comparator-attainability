# Claim scope and limitations

- One proper closed joint-convex comparator is chosen before sampling. The loss is bounded and the posterior may depend on the sample.
- The pointwise benchmark drops the binomial moment term. The comparison concerns the expectation of generic upper inversions, including when the comparator's infimum is unattained.
- The two-sample law has a positive lower gap and a Catoni upper estimate. The ordinary KL comparator supplies a distribution-free upper estimate.
- The exact gap for the example and for arbitrary dataset laws is not determined. The proof does not address removing the moment penalty by a different high-probability proof.
- Python and partial Lean cover the exact arithmetic. The analytical moment argument and risk inversion estimates are written proofs.
