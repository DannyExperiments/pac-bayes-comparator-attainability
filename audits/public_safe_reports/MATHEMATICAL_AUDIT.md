# Mathematical review

In the constructed law both hypotheses make identical predictions on the supported data. Varying the posterior according to the label count gives exactly the stated KL shifts and leaves the empirical risk unchanged. The first two ideal inversions are both 3/4. The third is 1. The normalized moment and convex slice inequality imply a pointwise floor, so an excess in one branch cannot be offset by another. The two selected terms exceed one if both inversions are below the stated root. The two upper estimates follow from fixed Catoni and ordinary KL comparators respectively.
